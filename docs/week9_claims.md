# Week 9 claims ledger

The authoritative, generated ledger is
[the publication claims document](../artifacts/week9_publication/claims.md).
Its [JSON record](../artifacts/week9_publication/claims.json) retains exact
source paths, SHA-256 digests, row selectors, columns, numerical values,
uncertainty, denominators and qualifications; the
[CSV view](../artifacts/week9_publication/claims.csv) uses a stable column order.

The 24 records distinguish supported and descriptive findings, shared
expected-objective targets under stated assumptions, and explicitly excluded
claims about matched first-order theory and general dependent-gap covariance.
Their numerical text is regenerated and checked from the pinned source evidence.
The [completion report](week9_completion.md) links the actual validation and
freeze evidence.
